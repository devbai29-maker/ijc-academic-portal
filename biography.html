Biography of My Hero: Alan Turing

Alan Turing (1912–1954) was a brilliant mathematician, logician, and cryptanalyst who laid the foundational concepts of modern theoretical computer science and artificial intelligence.

During World War II, Turing played a pivotal role in cracking intercepted coded messages, including the German Enigma machine at Bletchley Park, saving millions of lives and shaping modern computing technology.
